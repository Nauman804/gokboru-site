Shipping & Returns

Last updated: January 2026

Shipping

Every Meridian 01 is shipped fully insured, in discreet, tamper-evident packaging. Delivery timelines are confirmed individually with each owner following allocation, as pieces are regulated and finished to order.

  • Signature required on delivery.
  • Full tracking provided from dispatch.
  • International delivery available — duties and taxes are the responsibility of the recipient unless otherwise agreed.

Returns

Given the limited, individually numbered nature of this collection, returns are handled on a case-by-case basis through our atelier.

  • Contact us within 14 days of delivery to initiate a return enquiry.
  • The piece must be unworn, with all original packaging, papers, and the numbered certificate intact.
  • Return shipping is arranged and insured by GOKBORU.

Warranty

Every Meridian 01 carries a five-year international warranty covering manufacturing defects in movement and case. The warranty does not cover damage from misuse, water exposure beyond rating, or unauthorized servicing.

Contact

For any shipping or return enquiry, reach our atelier at atelier@gokboru.com.
